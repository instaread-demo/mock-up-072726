Hannah Rapp grew up in Yorktown, and the community watched with pride as she pursued her dreams as a student, athlete, Purdue graduate, and professional boxer.  Following her tragic passing last weekend, tributes have poured in from across the country and throughout the boxing community, including from her recent world title opponent, Tiara Brown, who shared that before they stepped into the ring, Hannah asked for her autograph. When asked about her opponent, Brown described Hannah as “the best dance partner I have ever faced as a pro.” Community members are organizing a candlelight vigil in Hannah’s honor, timed to coincide with one being held in Texas – tonight (Wednesday, July 22) at 9:30pm, at Morrow’s Meadow Pavilion. Bright colors are encouraged in celebration of Hannah’s life.

43-year-old James W. Jackson was arrested Monday morning after his vehicle went off I-69 during a high-speed chase with Indiana Excise Police. The Star Press says Jackson is a homicide suspect in Wyoming, Michigan, and the chase reached speeds of 100 mph.

Just three Anderson residents spoke during an Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission hearing Monday to allow public comment on a proposed electric rate hike. The Herald Bulletin says The Indiana Office of Utility Consumer Counselor will make recommendations about the hike on July 31.

This Saturday, (July 25th) Muncie Central High School will host the 25th Annual Marching Band Contest — it will bring together multiple high school bands from across the state, with over 2,100 spectators, 1,500 talented student performers, and 250 dedicated band staff joining us on campus. Due to the scale of this event — which will utilize the Fieldhouse, stadium complex, high school, and surrounding green spaces — The Muncie YMCA will close at 3pm Saturday. 24/7 Access members can choose to to use the Yorktown YMCA after 3pm.

A celebration of life has been scheduled for the late Dr. John C. Peterson. July 28 events begin at the Horizon Convention Center with doors open at 1 p.m., Pre-service at 1:30 p.m., and at 2:30 pm the Memorial Service. Then, everyone’s invited to The Landing at Redwing 5757 E Inlow Springs Rd, Muncie, IN – 5 pm Gates open, with event from 6 pm – 10 pm with Live music Food trucks onsite.

The Anderson Community Schools Board of Trustees has unanimously approved an update to its original policy by banning all electronic devices from school buildings. The Herald Bulletin says the new language comes in the wake of the state’s “bell-to-bell” ban during the school day.

53-year-old Brian Lee Blevins Sr. has been granted early release after being sentenced to 40 years in prison for conspiracy to commit murder in 2015. Judge Doug Mawhorr made the decision in part because of good behavior and the completion of several “education modules” while imprisoned.

The 26th annual Hog Roast hosted by the CLC Historical Preservation Society will take place Saturday from 4:30 to 7 p.m. at Howell Farms near Middletown. Dinner will be served in the historic Josiah Cromer Homestead Barn, which was built around 1857.

South Madison Schools superintendent Dr. Mark Hall had exciting news to share last week with “The Anderson Journal” morning show. All three of the district’s elementary schools were invited to the state capital to celebrate their I-Read 3 scores.

August is Child Support Awareness Month, and Delaware County prosecutor Eric Hoffman is reminding residents the full range of prosecutor-provided services is available to all Indiana parents and guardians free of charge.

Chesterfield has a boil water advisory. Reporter Sean Mattingly saw the info on the fire department sign yesterday, and believes roadside digging has caused the precaution.

The Salvation Army of Muncie will hold a purse auction on August 8 from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at 1015 North Wheeling Avenue. Food and refreshments will be provided. Call (765) 289-7924 for more information or email Kathleen.pinkston@usc.salvationarmy.org.
