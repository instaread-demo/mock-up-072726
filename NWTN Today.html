Anna McMillin

Mrs. Anna McMillin, 87, a resident of Union City, died July 21, 2026, at Baptist Memorial Hospital-Union City.
Services will be held at 2 p.m. on Sunday July, 26, 2026, at White-Ranson Funeral Home. Rev. Phillip Pinion will officiate. Burial will follow in Obion County Memorial Gardens.
Pallbearers will be David McMillin, David Jeffress, Jeff McMillin, Randy Smith, Joe Usrey and Nolan Jeffress.
Honorary pallbearers will be Dwight Grace, Bobby Wheelis, Michael Atkins, J.J. Chandler, Skipper White, David Ginn and Dale Vincent.
The family will receive friends from noon-2 p.m. on Sunday at the White-Ranson Funeral Home.
Mrs. McMillin was born on Dec. 20, 1938, in Cuba, Ky., to the late Finis and Roselean (Lee) Usrey. She worked as a Floral Designer for the former Calla Lily and Flowers With Sass. On Oct. 6, 1960, she married John “Tom” McMillin. He preceded her in death on July 6, 2000.
She was a member of Union City Cumberland Presbyterian Church. Mrs. Anna volunteered in the Baptist Hospital Gift Shop.
The family requests memorials be sent to Union City Cumberland Presbyterian Church.
She is survived by a sister, Bonnie Jeffress of Fulton; sister-in-law, Judy Usrey of Martin; and several nieces and nephews.
She was also preceded in death by her son, Charles “Chuck” McMillin; brother, Jimmie L. Usrey; and her brother-in-law, Michael Jeffress.
Condolences may be sent to the family at the funeral home website: www.white-ranson.com.
