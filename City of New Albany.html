    Stars, Stripes, & Second Farewells: Flag Drop Box at Village Hall

    • July 9, 2026
    Listen to audio version of this article
    Sponsored by
    0:00 / 0:45
    Instaread

    Every American flag tells a story. When it’s time for that story to end, the City of New Albany can help ensure it receives a dignified farewell. Our flag retirement drop box is now located on Village Hall Road near the back entrance to Village Hall (99 E. Main Street). Residents can drop off worn, torn, or faded American flags for proper retirement.

    Did you know that the American Legion has promoted public flag retirement ceremonies since 1937 as a way to honor flags that have faithfully served our nation? If your flag is ready to come down, we’re here to help give it the respectful send-off it deserves.
