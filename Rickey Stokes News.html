COWARTS:    Houston County 911 dispatched Ashford Rescue, Houston County Sheriff Deputies and Ashford Police to a medical emergency in Cowarts. Reports of a child not breathing.

The child was on a feeding tube and not breathing. Ashford Rescue mutual aid requested Dothan Fire Engine 5, eastside.

On arrival it was a load and go from the scene to the hospital. Houston County Sheriff Deputies did an emergency escort of the ambulance to the hospital to allow the ambulance a no slow down.
